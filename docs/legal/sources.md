## Notes per source

### 1. Open-Meteo
- Terms page: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms. Licence page: https://open-meteo.com/en/licence.
- Upstream GloFAS and Copernicus licences are linked from the licence page and were not read. Open: read them before we cite GloFAS values as such.
- The terms do not address storing or caching. Open: ask info@open-meteo.com if we keep copies beyond the demo.

### 2. Copernicus EMS EMSR773
- Activation facts from the public rapid-mapping API: "Flood in Valencia Region, Spain", event 2024-10-29 14:30 UTC, activation 2024-10-29 16:40 UTC, requested by Spain's Ministry of Interior (CENEM). AOI 1 is Valencia province. Product type DEL (delineation, monitoring). Source: https://rapidmapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/backend/dashboard-api/public-activations/?code=EMSR773
- Activation page: https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/activations/EMSR773
- Terms: https://emergency.copernicus.eu (Terms and Conditions, last modified 07/06/2023). Granted: reproduction, distribution, communication to the public, adaptation and combination. When we communicate or distribute CEMS data we must tell recipients the source, using the Citation Guidelines.
- No warranty. We must not present the data as guaranteed to be error-free. Show it with the UI disclaimer "for information purposes only".
- Modified or combined data: independent IP we create stays ours. EU keeps its rights in the original data.
- Citation Guidelines (page updated 20 Aug 2026). The gameplan string `Generated using ...` is **not** what they ask for. Use the formats below:
  - Activation: `Copernicus Emergency Management Service (© 2024 European Union), EMSR773` (activation year is 2024).
  - A specific layer: `Copernicus Emergency Management Service (© 2024 European Union), [EMSR773] <AOI name> (AOI0n): <Product Type>`, for example `[EMSR773] Horta Sud (AOI03): Delineation Map`.
  - Hyperlink the activation to its page: https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/activations/EMSR773
